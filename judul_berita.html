He Scammed the Book Business for Years.
Is He Back?

11 May, 2010

The man who admitted to stealing hundreds of manuscripts swore he’d never do it again.
New online skulduggery resembles his old tricks, and then some.

Last month, the author Catherine Lacey received an email whose subject line quoted from the beginning of her next novel, “Gemma.” The message contained a passage from the unfinished manuscript followed by a wall of 48 middle finger emojis.
